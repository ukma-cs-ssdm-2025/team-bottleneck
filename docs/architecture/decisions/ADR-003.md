# ADR-003: Payment Integration

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2025-09-28  

## Context
- **FR-002**: card payment (Stripe).  
- **FR-007**: transaction history storage.  
- **NFR-002**: secure storage of payment data.  

## Decision
Use a third-party payment service (Stripe).  
Card data must be stored in encrypted form.  

## Consequences
- ✅ High level of security.  
- ✅ Support for multiple payment methods.  
- ⚠️ Additional transaction fees.  
- ❌ Dependency on an external provider.  

## Considered Alternatives
- **In-house payment processing** — rejected due to high security risks and expensive PCI DSS certification.
